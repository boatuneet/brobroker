## Why

High-ticket brokers lose deal momentum because critical knowledge is scattered across calls, notes, PDFs, inboxes, listing documents, and memory. The opportunity is to build an AI Deal Brain that gives brokers perfect recall, faster qualification, better buyer-to-asset matching, and premium client communication without positioning the product as another generic CRM.

The first product wedge should focus on yacht brokers, because yacht assets are complex, international, document-heavy, high-value, and highly sensitive to missed follow-up or poor qualification.

## What Changes

- Build the first MVP foundation for an AI Deal Brain for high-ticket brokers, optimized initially for yacht brokerage workflows.
- Introduce living asset intelligence for listings, including specs, documents, notes, comps, FAQs, objections, missing information, and broker-ready answers.
- Introduce buyer and seller memory that captures preferences, objections, urgency, relationship notes, rejected assets, communication style, and next actions.
- Introduce a voice-to-CRM capture flow that turns natural broker call notes into structured profile updates, tasks, pipeline updates, and follow-up drafts.
- Introduce human-approved follow-up generation across email, WhatsApp/SMS-style messages, call summaries, viewing recaps, and negotiation updates.
- Introduce buyer-to-asset matching and client brief matching that produce exact matches, near matches, smart substitutes, fit rationale, missing criteria, and broker talking points.
- Introduce a verified buyer gate that classifies inquiries as Verified, Needs Review, or High Risk using mockable verification signals and an audit-friendly explanation.
- Introduce seller update reports that summarize inquiries, buyer quality, viewing activity, objections, market context, and next-week actions.
- Introduce private buyer deal rooms with shortlists, comparisons, rationale, approved documents, itinerary details, and a constrained Q&A experience.
- Introduce a deal timeline / next-best-action dashboard for hot buyers, stale leads, overdue follow-ups, missing documents, owner updates, and verification status.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `asset-intelligence`: Living asset knowledge base for yacht listings, source-backed answers, missing information, objections, and broker pitch support.
- `client-memory`: Buyer and seller memory profiles, including preferences, relationship context, objections, rejected assets, urgency, and recommended next actions.
- `inquiry-verification`: Buyer and inquiry qualification gate with verification statuses, risk signals, proof-of-funds readiness, and audit-friendly explanations.
- `matching-shortlists`: Buyer-to-asset matching and client brief matching that generate ranked shortlists, close matches, smart substitutes, comparisons, fit rationale, and outreach suggestions.
- `broker-workflow-automation`: Voice-to-CRM note capture, follow-up drafting, seller update reports, deal timeline, and next-best-action workflow support.
- `buyer-deal-room`: Private buyer-facing deal room for shortlists, comparisons, approved materials, itinerary, financing links, and scoped Q&A.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Affected application surface: new broker workspace, listing intelligence views, client profile views, matching workflow, verification workflow, follow-up/report generation, next-best-action dashboard, and buyer deal room.
- Affected data model: assets/listings, documents, buyer profiles, seller profiles, conversations, notes, tasks, matches, verification checks, follow-up drafts, reports, deal rooms, and audit events.
- Affected integrations: AI model provider for extraction/generation/matching, future CRM/calendar/email/WhatsApp/SMS integrations, and future KYC/KYB/AML vendors such as Persona, Trulioo, Middesk, Sumsub, ComplyAdvantage, or Plaid Assets.
- Initial implementation should support a clickable 7-day prototype with realistic sample yacht listings and mock external verification signals before investing in regulated vendor integrations.
